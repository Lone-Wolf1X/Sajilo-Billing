Bhai, ye accounting ka bahut important concept hai. ❤️

Sabse pehle ek baat samajh: Profit aur Cash Flow dono alag cheezein hain.

- Profit batata hai ki business ne kitna kamaya ya kitna loss kiya.
- Cash Flow batata hai ki business mein actual cash aur bank money kahan se aaya aur kahan gaya.

Ek business profit mein ho sakta hai, lekin uske paas cash nahi ho. Aur kabhi business loss mein hote hue bhi uske paas cash ho sakta hai.

Chal, apni wahi Rs. 20 lakh wali Electronics Shop ka example continue karte hain.

# 3. Cash Flow mein kya count hoga?

Sabse important classification:

## A. Operating Activities

Ye business ki daily operations se related cash inflow aur outflow hain.

| Transaction             | Cash Flow |
| ----------------------- | --------- |
| Cash Sales              | +1,50,000 |
| Bank Sales              | +2,00,000 |
| Cash Purchase           | -2,00,000 |
| Supplier Payment        | -1,00,000 |
| Rent                    | -20,000   |
| Salary                  | -30,000   |
| Electricity             | -5,000    |
| Interest Payment        | -10,000   |
| Net Operating Cash Flow | -15,000   |

Credit purchase Rs. 3 lakh aur credit sale Rs. 1 lakh ko abhi cash flow mein include nahi karenge, kyunki actual payment ya receipt nahi hui.

## B. Investing Activities

Business ke long-term assets ki buying aur selling.

| Transaction             | Cash Flow |
| ----------------------- | --------- |
| New Furniture Purchase  | -50,000   |
| New Computer Purchase   | -30,000   |
| Old Equipment Sale      | +10,000   |
| Net Investing Cash Flow | -70,000   |

## C. Financing Activities

Business mein capital aur loan se related cash movements.

| Transaction              | Cash Flow |
| ------------------------ | --------- |
| New Bank Loan Received   | +0        |
| Owner Additional Capital | +0        |
| Loan Principal Repayment | -20,000   |
| Net Financing Cash Flow  | -20,000   |

Yahan new loan aur additional capital zero hain kyunki is month koi naya loan ya capital nahi aaya.

## 5. Cash Flow actual mein hume kya batata hai?

Maan le business owner tu hi hai. Ab tu Cash Flow Statement dekhkar ye 5 cheezein samajh sakta hai:

1. Business se actual cash generate ho raha hai ya nahi?

Operating Cash Flow = Rs. -15,000.

Matlab is month daily business operations se jitna paisa aaya, usse Rs. 15,000 zyada cash business se bahar gaya.

2. Business apna supplier payment kar pa raha hai ya nahi?

Agar suppliers ko payment karna hai aur cash available nahi hai, toh business ko working capital ki problem ho sakti hai.

3. Business assets kharidne mein kitna paisa laga raha hai?

Investing Cash Flow = Rs. -70,000.

Matlab business ne existing cash se additional long-term assets mein investment kiya.

4. Business loan par kitna dependent hai?

Financing Activities se pata chalta hai ki business owner se capital le raha hai, bank se loan le raha hai ya loan repay kar raha hai.

5. Business ke paas actual kitna cash available hai?

Closing Cash & Bank = Rs. 1,95,000.

Ye owner ko liquidity position samajhne mein help karta hai.

# 6. Profit vs Cash Flow – sabse important difference

Maan le business ne Rs. 1 lakh ka maal credit par sell kiya.

| Particulars          | Amount       |
| -------------------- | ------------ |
| Sales Revenue        | Rs. 1,00,000 |
| Cost of Goods Sold   | Rs. 70,000   |
| Gross Profit         | Rs. 30,000   |
| Actual Cash Received | Rs. 0        |

Ab dekh:

- Income Statement mein Rs. 1 lakh revenue show hoga.
- Profit mein Rs. 30,000 gross profit show hoga.
- Cash Flow mein abhi Rs. 0 cash inflow show hoga.
- Balance Sheet mein Rs. 1 lakh Accounts Receivable show hoga.

Yahi reason hai ki Profit aur Cash Flow alag financial statements hain.

3 important accounting rules:

1. Bank se cash transfer aur cash se bank transfer ko cash flow mein income ya expense nahi count karna. Ye internal transfers hain.
2. Depreciation non-cash expense hai. Indirect Cash Flow Method mein ise net profit mein add back kiya jata hai.
3. Loan principal repayment financing activity hai, jabki interest classification applicable accounting policy ke according consistently maintain honi chahiye.
